Frequently Asked Questions

Is GarudAI free to use?

Yes, absolutely. GarudAI is a civic initiative built to empower citizens. Generating complaints and finding department emails will always remain 100% free.

Do I need to type the exact address?

No. When you submit an issue, GarudAI automatically requests your browser's GPS location and reverse-geocodes it into a Ward, City, and Pincode.

Which AI model powers the backend?

We use the highly optimized Llama-3-8B model running on Groq's LPUs, allowing us to generate complex JSON responses in milliseconds.

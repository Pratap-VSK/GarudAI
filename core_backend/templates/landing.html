Standard Operating Procedure

Smart Civic Resolution Process

A transparent, automated pipeline built to close the gap between citizens and municipal authorities without any bureaucratic delays.

01

Smart Location Mapping

Your device's GPS captures precise coordinates, securely cross-referencing them against official municipal ward boundaries to pinpoint your exact local jurisdiction.

02

Intelligent Categorization

Our dedicated AI engine instantly analyzes your incident description, determining the exact responsible internal department in milliseconds.

03

Automated Dispatch

A government-compliant formal grievance is dynamically drafted and electronically dispatched directly to the designated authority — ensuring full transparency.
